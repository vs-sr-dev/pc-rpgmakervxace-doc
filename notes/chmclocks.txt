  hdr-vs-c4    : the ITSF header's truncated u32 at +0x10, joined
                 to the code-4 FILETIME's high half, minus that
                 FILETIME. Two structures written by one pass.
  code10-code4 : the gap three earlier specimens read as the
                 compile duration. Where it is a whole number of
                 hours plus a few seconds it is DECOMPOSED, because
                 a constant and a duration are not one quantity.

  No time zone in the Windows or IANA databases lies outside
  -12:00 .. +14:00, so a whole-hour term larger than fourteen
  cannot be one field being local while the other is UTC.
