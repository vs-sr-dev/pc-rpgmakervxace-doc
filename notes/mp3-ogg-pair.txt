  An MPEG audio stream is a whole number of fixed-length frames,
  so its derived duration can only ever OVERSHOOT the material it
  encodes -- by up to one frame of padding, plus whatever encoder
  delay the last frame carries. A Vorbis stream declares its
  sample count exactly. So a positive difference of two to three
  frame-times in every pair is what two encodings of one piece of
  audio look like, and a NEGATIVE one would falsify that.
  23 of 23 are positive.

  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SAY. Two streams of equal duration
  are two streams of equal DURATION. This tool decodes not one
  audio sample, so it cannot say the two files hold the same
  music, and the word does not appear in its output.
